## Parties

**MyGravelGuy** ("MGG"), a [entity type TBD — confirm with counsel] operating
mygravelguy.com, and **[Partner Yard Legal Name]** ("Partner"), a Texas [entity type],
doing business at [partner address].

## Scope

Partner agrees to fulfill delivery orders for gravel, mulch, sand, and/or soil products
placed through MyGravelGuy's website within the agreed DFW delivery zone(s):
`dfw-core` (Dallas & Tarrant counties) / `dfw-north` (Collin & Denton) / `dfw-outer`
(Rockwall, Kaufman, Ellis & Johnson) — strike zones not covered. MGG remains the
merchant of record for every order; Partner is an independent fulfillment contractor,
not an employee, agent, or joint venturer of MGG.

## Ordering & dispatch process

1. MGG receives and confirms the customer's order, address, material, quantity, and
   delivery date, and collects payment (Stripe card authorization hold).
2. MGG transmits the order to Partner via [dispatch method to be agreed — phone, text,
   email, or a shared order system] with the delivery address, material, quantity, and
   requested date/window.
3. **Confirmation SLA:** Partner confirms acceptance (or declines, stating why) within
   [X hours — suggest 2 business hours during Partner's stated hours] of receiving the
   order.
4. Partner delivers within the confirmed window, photographs the placed material, and
   marks the order delivered (method TBD — MGG's existing `/delivery-confirm` flow, or a
   text/email back to MGG).
5. MGG captures the Stripe payment hold and pays Partner per the terms below.

## Pricing

Partner provides a price sheet (see `price-sheet-template.csv`) covering pickup price
per unit, delivered price or delivery fee per MGG zone, truck sizes/max load, minimum
load, Saturday surcharge, and lead time, for each material Partner stocks. **Price
changes require [30] days' written notice to MGG** before taking effect on new orders;
orders already confirmed at the old price are honored at that price.

## Payment terms

MGG pays Partner **[net-7 from delivery confirmation / weekly ACH batch — pick one]**
for all confirmed, delivered orders. Disputed orders (wrong material, damage claim, etc.)
are paid on the undisputed portion while the disputed portion is resolved per the
Quality section below. [Payment method: ACH / check — TBD.]

## Delivery standards

- Delivery within the confirmed date/window; Partner notifies MGG (or the customer
  directly, per an agreed method) of any delay as soon as known.
- **Photo proof of drop** at every delivery, showing the material placed at the location
  requested.
- Material is placed at the customer's direction (driveway spot, drop pin, or verbal
  instruction relayed by MGG) — Partner is not responsible for spreading/grading unless
  separately agreed and priced.
- **Driveway/property damage waiver:** the customer accepts responsibility for
  driveway/surface damage from a placement they direct, disclosed to the customer at
  checkout; Partner still carries its own auto/GL insurance (see Insurance) for driver-
  caused damage outside the customer's direction.

## Quality

- Material must match the agreed product spec on the price sheet (grade, color,
  moisture/contamination level).
- **Contamination or wrong-material remedy:** if a delivered load is confirmed wrong or
  contaminated (e.g. unfinished organic matter in a soil/compost blend, wrong grade of
  stone), Partner will, at MGG's election, either **re-deliver a corrected load at no
  additional charge** or **issue a full credit** for that order within [5] business
  days of the complaint being substantiated (photo evidence from the customer,
  reasonable good-faith review).
- For soil/compost/garden-mix products specifically, MGG may request a current
  certificate of analysis or third-party test result (e.g. US Composting Council Seal of
  Testing Assurance, if held) on a reasonable, periodic basis.

## Cancellations & weather

Either party may cancel or reschedule an order up to [X hours] before the confirmed
window at no charge. Weather-related delays are not a breach by either party; Partner
notifies MGG as soon as a weather delay is known so MGG can update the customer.

## Insurance

Partner maintains, at its own expense: commercial auto liability covering delivery
vehicles, and commercial general liability (CGL) covering its operations, each at
limits MGG reasonably requests (e.g. $1M/$2M), and names **MyGravelGuy as an additional
insured** with a current **certificate of insurance (COI)** on file with MGG, renewed
annually or on any policy change.

## Non-solicitation of MGG customers

For the term of this agreement and [12] months after, Partner agrees not to directly
solicit repeat business from a customer whose order it learned of solely through MGG,
using contact information obtained through fulfilling that order — and MGG agrees not to
solicit Partner's existing customers using information Partner discloses about them.
**Texas enforceability caveat:** non-solicitation/non-compete provisions are subject to
Texas Business & Commerce Code Chapter 15 (Texas Free Enterprise and Antitrust Act) and
Covenants Not to Compete Act reasonableness requirements (scope, geography, duration) —
have counsel confirm this clause is reasonable and enforceable before relying on it, and
keep it mutual and narrowly time-limited as drafted here, not broader.

## Branding & co-marketing

Partner may mention that it fulfills MyGravelGuy online orders, **including on its own
Google Business Profile**, only with **MGG's prior written consent** to the specific
language used (per the owner's decision: no MGG-branded GBP presence in DFW; any
partner mention of MGG online ordering requires the partner's — and here, also MGG's —
mutual consent to how it's phrased). MGG may name Partner publicly as a fulfilling yard
for MyGravelGuy DFW orders **only with Partner's prior written consent**, on the same
mutual-consent basis. Neither party may imply the other is its employer, subsidiary, or
exclusive partner without agreement in writing.

## Confidentiality

Each party keeps the other's pricing, order volume, and customer information
confidential, using it only to perform this agreement, for the term and [2] years after.

## Term & termination

This agreement begins on the date both parties sign and continues until either party
terminates with **30 days' written notice**. Either party may terminate immediately for
the other's material breach (e.g. repeated missed delivery windows, non-payment,
repeated quality failures) not cured within [10] business days of written notice.

## Governing law

This agreement is governed by the laws of the **State of Texas**, without regard to
conflict-of-law principles. [Venue/dispute-resolution clause — arbitration vs. court,
county — TBD with counsel.]
