# MyGravelGuy — Marketing Strategy, Target Demographics & Google Ads Plan

## Business Objectives (Q1-Q2 2026)

1. **Primary**: Acquire more contractor accounts (higher AOV, repeat business)
2. **Secondary**: Drive homeowner orders via Google Ads (volume play)
3. **Increase quote-to-order conversion rate**
4. **Build brand recognition as "the nationwide gravel delivery company"**

---

## Target Demographics

### Segment 1: Contractors (PRIMARY TARGET)

#### Profile
- **Who**: Landscapers, general contractors, excavation companies, playground/turf installers, hardscape companies
- **Company size**: 2-50 employees, $500K-$10M revenue
- **Geography**: Work across multiple states or frequently travel outside their home market
- **Key trait**: They already have local suppliers at home, but need a reliable source when working in unfamiliar markets

#### Why They Need Us
- Working a job 3 states away and don't know local quarries
- Need 20 tons of crusher run delivered to a jobsite by next Tuesday
- Don't have time to call around and negotiate with unknown suppliers
- Want one reliable contact they can call/order from anywhere

#### Typical Order
- 15-50+ tons
- AOV: $2,000-$5,000+
- Products: Crusher run, road base, crushed stone, pea gravel, topsoil, mulch
- Frequency: Monthly or per-project basis

#### Where to Find Them
- Google Search: "bulk gravel delivery [city]", "aggregate supplier near me", "commercial gravel delivery"
- Apollo.io: Email outreach to landscaping companies, GCs, excavation firms
- Industry directories, contractor associations
- LinkedIn targeting

#### Messaging That Resonates
- "One call for all your material needs, anywhere in the US"
- "Do you have a gravel guy? Now you do."
- "Stop calling around to local quarries in every new city"
- "Nationwide aggregate sourcing — one point of contact"
- "We handle the logistics so you can focus on the job"

#### Contractor Subtypes to Target

| Subtype | Materials They Need | Why They Need Us |
|---|---|---|
| **Playground/Turf Installers** | Pea gravel, sand, crushed stone, drainage rock | Install playgrounds nationally, need materials in every city |
| **Landscaping Companies** | Mulch, topsoil, decorative stone, river rock | Large residential/commercial projects in varying locations |
| **General Contractors** | Crusher run, road base, fill dirt, crushed concrete | Site work across regional/national projects |
| **Excavation/Grading** | Fill dirt, road base, crushed stone | Large quantity needs, often in remote areas |
| **Hardscapers** | Paver base, decomposed granite, crushed stone | Patio/driveway installs across markets |

### Segment 2: Homeowners (SECONDARY TARGET)

#### Profile
- **Who**: Homeowners doing DIY or hiring contractors for landscaping/driveway projects
- **Age**: 30-65
- **Income**: $75K+
- **Geography**: Suburban and rural (homes with yards/driveways)
- **Key trait**: Don't know where to get bulk materials or how much they need

#### Why They Need Us
- "I need gravel for my driveway but don't know where to start"
- Can't pick up 5 tons of gravel in their truck
- Want instant pricing without calling around
- Need help figuring out what material and how much

#### Typical Order
- 3-10 tons
- AOV: $400-$1,200
- Products: Driveway gravel, pea gravel, mulch, topsoil, river rock, sand
- Frequency: 1-2x per year (seasonal)

#### Where to Find Them
- Google Search: "gravel delivery near me", "how much gravel for driveway", "bulk mulch delivery"
- Google Shopping: product-specific searches
- Home improvement blogs/content
- Social media (Facebook, Instagram)

#### Messaging That Resonates
- "Bulk gravel delivered to your driveway — instant pricing"
- "Calculate how much you need in 30 seconds"
- "Free delivery included in every price"
- "Order online, delivered this week"

## Apollo.io Email Sequencing Strategy (Contractors)

### Current Setup
- Apollo.io website tracker installed (captures visitor company info)
- Used for email outreach sequences to contractors

### Recommended Sequences

#### Sequence 1: Cold Outreach — Landscaping Companies
**Target**: Landscaping companies with 5+ employees, multi-state presence
**Subject lines**:
- "Do you have a gravel guy?"
- "Quick question about your material sourcing"
- "Nationwide aggregate delivery — one contact for every jobsite"

**Steps**:
1. Day 0: Introduction email (who we are, value prop)
2. Day 3: Follow-up with case study / testimonial
3. Day 7: "What materials do you typically need?"
4. Day 14: Final touch — direct link to contractor page

#### Sequence 2: Cold Outreach — General Contractors
**Target**: GCs with projects in multiple states
**Angle**: "Stop wasting time sourcing materials in unfamiliar markets"

#### Sequence 3: Warm Leads — Quote Follow-Up
**Target**: People who submitted quotes but didn't convert
**Steps**:
1. Day 1: "Your quote is ready" (if not already sent)
2. Day 3: "Any questions about your quote?"
3. Day 7: "Limited-time pricing" / urgency
4. Day 14: "Still need materials?"

#### Sequence 4: Re-engagement — Past Customers
**Target**: Customers who ordered once but haven't reordered
**Steps**:
1. "How did your project turn out?"
2. "Your next order: exclusive pricing"
